# Define the concept matrix and conflict-ruling method

Type: prototype
Status: open
Blocked by: 09, 11, 12

## Question

How are cards from different sources set side by side, and how is each conflict ruled? Prototype the cross-source concept matrix (how cards are grouped into one concept, how each group is marked consensus / complement / conflict, and where a group cites its cards) and the shape of a conflict ruling under `workshop/charter.md` ("How to apply it": hard constraints, then rankings, then bring uncovered conflicts to the user). Build it from real cards out of at least three sources, so it shows real overlaps and conflicts. Cards come from the Answer of [Define the concept card schema and extraction method](03-define-the-concept-card-schema-and-extraction-method.md).
